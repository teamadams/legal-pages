1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Team Adams (“we,” “us”) handles information in connection with the private software integration known as Soren Oura Integration (the “Integration”). The Integration is an internal personal tool used by Felipe Adams and authorized Team Adams operator systems (including Soren). It is not offered as a public consumer product and is not available for general signup.

2. Who this applies to

This policy applies to the Oura account holder who authorizes the Integration (Felipe Adams) and to wearable/health metrics accessed through that authorization.

3. Information we access

When authorized, the Integration may access Oura API data necessary for personal health and recovery workflows, which may include:

  • personal profile metadata associated with the Oura account;
  • daily readiness, sleep, activity, and stress summaries;
  • heart-rate, HRV-related, SpO2, workout, session, and heart-health metrics when scoped;
  • tags and related user-entered Oura annotations when scoped; and
  • technical connection metadata such as token status, timestamps, and operational logs.

The Integration is not intended to publicly publish health data or to provide data to advertisers.

4. How we use information

We use accessed information solely to:

  • sync personal wearable metrics into Felipe’s private health system;
  • generate personal recovery, training, and wellness insights for Felipe;
  • maintain connection health, security, and audit logs for the Integration; and
  • support related personal health record-keeping requested by Felipe.

We do not sell personal health data. We do not use Oura data for advertising.

5. How we store and protect information

Credentials and tokens associated with the Integration are stored in controlled secret stores used by Team Adams / Soren operations (including 1Password and approved local secure storage). Health extracts are stored in private, access-controlled systems. Public websites hosting this policy do not store Oura metric payloads.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data accessed through the Integration. Data may be processed by infrastructure and model providers strictly as needed to operate Felipe’s authorized private systems, subject to Team Adams access and egress controls. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, security, or property of Team Adams and its operators.

7. Retention

We retain health metrics, derived summaries, and operational logs for as long as needed for Felipe’s personal health tracking and system operations, or until deleted/revoked by Felipe. Connection credentials may be rotated or revoked at any time.

8. Your choices and control

Felipe may disconnect the Integration, revoke Oura authorization, rotate client credentials, and delete local connection materials subject to any personal recordkeeping choices he makes.

9. Children

The Integration is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published at this URL.
