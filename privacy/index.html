1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Refuge Financial LLC (“Refuge,” “we,” “us”) handles information in connection with the private software integration known as Soren QuickBooks Online Integration (the “Integration”). The Integration is an internal tool used by authorized operators of Refuge and Team Adams. It is not offered as a public consumer product and is not available for general signup.

2. Who this applies to

This policy applies to:

  • authorized operators who connect the Integration to a QuickBooks Online company; and
  • business and accounting data accessed through that authorized connection.

The Integration is intended only for Refuge-controlled QuickBooks Online companies and related Team Adams internal use.

3. Information we access

When authorized, the Integration may access QuickBooks Online accounting information necessary to operate Refuge bookkeeping workflows, which may include:

  • company profile and preferences;
  • chart of accounts;
  • customers, vendors, and related master records;
  • transactions such as invoices, bills, payments, purchases, journal entries, and attachments metadata;
  • standard accounting reports; and
  • technical connection metadata such as company/realm identifiers, token status, timestamps, and audit logs.

The Integration is configured for QuickBooks Online accounting scope. It is not intended to process Payments, Payroll filing submission, or Bill Pay / money-movement features unless separately and explicitly authorized.

4. How we use information

We use accessed information solely to:

  • read, reconcile, organize, and maintain Refuge accounting records;
  • produce internal reports, exports, and audit evidence;
  • support controlled bookkeeping workflows approved by Refuge operators; and
  • maintain security, connection health, and operational logs for the Integration.

We do not sell personal or financial data. We do not use QuickBooks data for advertising.

5. How we store and protect information

Credentials and tokens associated with the Integration are stored in controlled secret stores used by Team Adams / Soren operations (including 1Password and approved local secure storage). Access is limited to authorized systems and operators. We apply least-privilege access, audit logging, and revocable authorization practices.

Accounting extracts and working files may be stored in Refuge-controlled private systems used for family-office operations. Public websites hosting this policy do not store QuickBooks transaction data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data accessed through the Integration. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as needed to operate Refuge systems (for example, secure hosting, backup, or secrets management). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, security, or property of Refuge and its operators.

7. Retention

We retain accounting records, exports, and operational logs for as long as needed for bookkeeping, tax, audit, legal, and internal control purposes, or as otherwise required by applicable law and Refuge retention practices. Connection credentials may be rotated or revoked at any time by an authorized operator.

8. Your choices and control

Authorized operators may disconnect the Integration, revoke OAuth access through Intuit/QuickBooks, rotate client credentials, and delete or restrict local connection materials subject to legal and recordkeeping requirements.

9. Children

The Integration is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published at this URL.
